## [0.10.0] — 2026-09-05 — The character ships: attributes, a wallet, and a felt worth watching

Written from `git log --since=2026-09-05` (168 commits) and the branch reports in the commit bodies. Note the overlap: the 0.9.0 entry below was itself written mid-evening on 2026-09-05 from `--since=2026-08-29`, so it already names the day's early work (ATTR-1/2, AUTH-1, TEST-1, DSK2). This entry is the whole day.

- CHARACTER SYSTEM — ENGINE (ATTR-1, ATTR-3): six 0–100 attributes — READS, FOCUS, DISCIPLINE, COMPOSURE, DECEPTION, STAMINA — that change how well an agent executes the strategy he was given, never what he is told to do. `src/agent/attributes.js` with one hook per attribute at its call site, an `ATTRIBUTE_IMPACT` knob read live from env, and an `attrLog` ring buffer. ATTR-1d hinged `at()` at 50 so a neutral agent is exactly the pre-attribute agent at every impact setting — the single-line lerp had been a silent live-play change for every backfilled agent in prod. Eight zero-sum natures assigned at birth by a deterministic ladder off the draft profile (Nit → Rock, Loose Cannon → Hothead, TAG → Shark, Calling Station → Grinder), never re-rolled and never retro-rolled onto existing agents. Growth ticks are drawn once per attribute at session end from that attribute's own evidence; scouted bands narrow from hands played at 120/500/2000 and may only ever close. Fatigue is within-session state that erodes effective FOCUS and DISCIPLINE and is restored by time at the bar (RIDERS-1: one stage per 2h, and stored fatigue is no longer overwritten with `fresh` the moment an agent stands up). `attrCosts` on flagged hands name his misjudgment and never grade the hand; a mechanism that won the pot still gets a line, with `cost:false`. `scripts/verify-attributes.js` (knob-0 identity and neutral-is-neutral on all 15 rows) and `scripts/verify-growth.js` (600 hands over twelve sessions, offline, in about a second, unable to bill). Arena `--attributes off|low|mid|high|grow`.
- CHARACTER SYSTEM — MEASURED (1000 pairs each, real Haiku 4.5, reads on, impact 1, TAG vs TAG with seat B held at neutral 50): **high (80) = +36.83 bb/100 ±32.25** over 2000 hands, CI excluding zero; **low (25) = −23.88 bb/100 ±34.53**, CI including zero. Runs: `data/arena/run-2026-09-05T16-19-15-262Z.json` (high) and `run-2026-09-05T18-21-55-482Z.json` (low); the 50-pair runs earlier the same day are pilots and are pure noise (CI ±118 and ±211, direction reversed).
- CHARACTER SYSTEM — UI (ATTR-2, ATTR-2e): player card v2 on the profile with the six-bar cluster and a tapped focus panel (sparkline + big value + five-word caption); nature reveal and the birth card at creation; growth in the thread, a GREW badge on the roster and an attribute row in the hand review; worn posture at the felt and a fatigue row in the zoom; the river attribute panel in the watch analysis, rendered by both watch surfaces from one implementation; PlayerCardRail and the birth card in the desktop rail. Laws held throughout: the ceiling is a band width and never a number on a bar (printed once, in gold, only inside a tapped panel), no purchase path anywhere, every cost line reads as his misjudgment.
- WALLET AND POCKETS (WALLET-1, WUI-1..4): two balances, not one. The owner wallet is the player's money; each agent carries a pocket, and the size of the pocket picks the stakes he sits at — the pocket IS the bet, so there is no betting menu anywhere. `src/server/wallet.js` is pure money logic (ladder 10/20, 25/50, 50/100 at 100bb; fund/collect/auto-refill/buy-in/cash-out; float and realised P&L). Four funding modes — top-up, allowance, auto-refill and cut — with cut a first-class choice that asks for no amount, carries no guilt copy, and lets him keep his attributes, his read book and his grudges. Three owner-scoped routes (`GET /api/wallet`, `POST /api/agents/:id/fund`, `POST /api/agents/:id/collect`). SEED-1 migrates every existing agent to one buy-in in the pocket and the surplus in the wallet, reproducing today's runway exactly. Broke means "no way back without the owner", not "under a buy-in" — corrected when verify-server-life.js went red and the product, not the assertion, turned out to be wrong. UI: the wallet block and pocket rows on YOU, the full-screen funding sheet, the pocket line on the player card, the collect receipt drawn as a transfer and not a reward. `scripts/verify-wallet.js`, 107 checks, chip conservation re-asserted after every move.
- SQLITE STORE (SQLITE-1): `src/server/store.js` owns the connection and every table (profiles, agents, hands, opponent_stats, notification_state, wallets, meta; WAL, synchronous NORMAL). One new dependency, `better-sqlite3` pinned at 13.0.3 — synchronous by design, because every persistence path here is synchronous and an async driver would push `await` up through `recordHandResult` into table.js. Migration runs once on first open, imports the four JSON files and renames each to `.migrated` rather than deleting it; idempotent twice over. agentProfiles, handHistory, opponentStats and notifications now read and write through the store with no exported signature changed. `scripts/export-json.js` is the rollback path and was verified byte-identical on a JSON → SQLite → JSON round trip. `data/app.db` resolves from `process.cwd()`, and the isolation leak this exposed — verify-cache-headers.js spawning the real server at repo root and migrating the developer's own `data/agents.json` — is fixed and pinned by a regression test. VPS steps in `read-me-claude/DATA_MIGRATION.md`; design note in `docs/SQLITE_DESIGN.md`.
- WEB LOGIN (AUTH-1): one credential header, two signature schemes — the Mini App initData HMAC and the Telegram Login Widget payload — behind a single `verifyTelegramCredential()`, so every existing fetch site and the WS FLOOR_SUB path get the second door for free. Widget sessions expire after `LOGIN_MAX_AGE_S` (default 30 days). LoginGate for non-Mini-App browsers, `GET /api/auth/config` and `GET /api/auth/me`, a login link on the welcome page, and a logout row on YOU and in the desktop top bar. Nothing logs payloads or tokens.
- PACING, SERVER-SIDE (PACE-1): the CALM → HEATING → ALL-IN → SHOWDOWN ladder is server-authoritative and never derived client-side, so two watchers warm the felt on the same hand. `src/server/pace.js` is pure; the ladder only ever advances within a hand. **The staged hold is spectator-only** — watched, an all-in gets a 3–5s hold, the runout a card at a time ~700ms apart and the finished board held 2s; unwatched, the same hand resolves in 8ms, so watching is never the optimal way to play. Wire additions only: a `PACE` message plus `pace`, `potBb`, `heroEquity` and `reads` on the snapshot. Hero equity now rides every snapshot including the mid-hand catch-up one, computed once per (hand, board, seat). `scripts/verify-pace.js` drives two real sockets and asserts the beat with observed timings.
- WATCH v3 (W3-1..6, FIX-3): pacing states on the felt, the TugBar rope under the board carrying his equity, HeroRow3, and four tabs cut to two — LIVE ANALYSIS, RANGE and HISTORY removed with their code, because pot odds, fold equity and solver lines were the solver speaking over him. Haptics as the ww-ref's ten-row table, plus a sound layer shipped as a stub behind a switch. The prediction beat shipped dark behind a flag. - WATCH v4 (W4-1..5, SEAT-1): the DEAL beat — his two cards land 90ms apart, then the table's backs sweep out as one gesture; the warm state is reachable only by the proven owner, by construction, because it needs `heroHole`. Seats became characters: SeatGhost draws the same FloorGhost the casino floor uses, with the seat's own accent, and `seat.mood` now rides both seat projections so opponents stop standing neutral (SEAT-1a). Snapshots now go through a queue and come out no faster than a per-action dwell (fold 1500ms, check 900ms, bet/raise/call 1200ms, street 1800ms, showdown 4000ms) — a floor, not a schedule, draining at 2x past 12s behind live. The game, the decision, the pace frame and the chat travel as one bundle, so table talk can never appear before the action it was said about. Plus the muck (a folded seat throws its cards at the centre of the felt), the hand ceremony naming who won and for how much, "why the hand went wrong" pinned across the next deal, and one tap out of the ceremony into his thread carrying the hand id.
- MOOD AS A NUMBER (MOOD-2): mood became `{ state, heat 0–100, cause }` with the state read off the heat, because a 62 and a 94 were the same agent in the record. Event weights live in one table; COMPOSURE scales both directions; uneventful hands cool 2/hand, and the bar cools 10/hour and never below level. Owner messages are classified by lexicon — needle heats, care cools, small talk moves nothing — bounded at ±15 once per 10 hands, less than a won pot, so the table always outranks the thread and an owner cannot type an agent into tilt. The briefing, table talk and the felt all read the number, and the thread opens with his own line instead of a form letter. **The Mood Design Law is now enforced by a test**: the suite reads mood.js with comments and string literals stripped and fails on any absence-tracking identifier (neglect, abandoned, unopened, lastSeenAt, daysSince, lonely) or any subtraction of one clock reading from another. There is no guilt machinery in this product, and the build checks it rather than us remembering it.
