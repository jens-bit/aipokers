# Built phone playtest — 9 September 2026

Local build after batch45 repairs, 390×844, normal hand/routine timing, synthetic owners and saved generated identities. No paid model calls or external messages. These are observations of the real built client and server, not proof of production or live model quality.

## A new owner

I tapped **Draft your first agent** first because it was the clear way to put someone in the empty room. I chose “Tight and patient,” named him Pebble, and dealt him in. His card appeared about five seconds after opening the app. It named him a Rock and said, “Patient, you said. Good. I am a Rock.” The same character then arrived at Home.

I knew where he was: one home, nobody at the table. During the next two minutes he settled by the couch with a book. There was no unexplained automatic solo poker game. The room stayed usable and his body did not overlap another occupant. The bottom line stayed “I am ready. I will play what you gave me.” That is the existing Rock opener; it fits the cautious character, but it stayed unchanged throughout this short observation. This does not establish the quality of generated conversation.

My next likely tap was Pebble, to talk or place him, or the lit casino door. The small birth-card reveal followed by the same figure reading in the actual room was the moment I would show a friend. The meaning of “Nobody live” beside a living agent still feels ambiguous; this is recorded as a copy/design decision rather than silently changing the founder's requested live-count control.

## Four agents, one away, a Home game running

This reproduces the requested household state with synthetic Granite, Loose Cannon, Professor and Wild Card, not a copy of Jens's production data. The initial scene briefly said nobody was home before its roster arrived; by the table tap at0.6s it correctly said one at the casino and three home (BUG-157).

I tapped the kitchen table first because three agents were playing there. It offered Watch and Sit down. The sheet also said “0 chairs free” and that retirement was the only way to free a chair. That conflicts with the Sit action: it is counting agent slots while discussing a playing chair (BUG-154). The large blank felt in that sheet also needs comparison with its exact reference.

I closed the sheet with its visible cross and watched the room for two minutes. The three home agents played successive hands in distinct positions. Loose Cannon's lit wall frame showed his casino destination and changing result. I could tell who was away without opening another screen. His result went from positive to negative while the home game continued; two things happening in one household is the moment I would show a friend. Near two minutes Wild Card asked for beer, with Yes/Later/No below the room. The request did not cover the table or stack on a duplicate speech bubble.

Wild Card's bottom line then said “Deal me in. I am not here to wait around,” although he was already in the Home game. This is an existing nature opener used as a room fallback, not newly generated speech. It reads as a false present-tense request in this context (BUG-158). My next clear action was answering his actual beer request or tapping the away frame to watch Loose Cannon.

The first household observation accidentally left its table sheet open because the driver tried Escape. The second run used the actual visible Close control on a fresh scratch household and observed the unobscured room for the full120s. The first evidence is retained rather than represented as a clean Home observation.
