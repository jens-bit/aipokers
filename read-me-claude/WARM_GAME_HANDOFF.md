## What was wrong

Public Home Watch receives spectator seat `-1`. The old client rendered that as a foreground player, showing an anonymous body and unknown stack while obscuring the actual second player. The server already creates a deliberate solo Home game against a distinct House opponent such as Granite. This was a viewer presentation bug; no duplicate backend player was found and no extra House or game rule was introduced.

Watch now separates the visible camera seat from the authenticated action seat, selects an occupied player, displays their identity/name/stack and attaches decisions and speech to the actual actor. Public views say Watching and open a player read. Human controls and served-card privacy remain unchanged; empty and sparse snapshots do not fabricate a player.

Home's theme previously ended at its room. A single appearance owner now sets the document palette so lazy routes and body portals follow the same local clock/manual selection without remounting a game. Warm Day, Amber Dusk and Warm Night extend to Watch, human play, casino, draft, conversation, profile, wallet and replay surfaces. Warm Night uses charcoal, olive and walnut. Existing `night` preferences remain valid. Agent colors and classic card suits are independent of the lighting.
