Wave 63 · part A

The mark is the ghost at the rail.

A railbird is the one leaning on the rail watching somebody else play, with money on it and an opinion about every hand. That is the owner. He doesn’t play; he raises a player, backs him, sweats him and talks to him afterwards.

Wave 62’s birds are dropped, all of them, and the magpie with them. A bird has nothing to do with this game. What does is the character everybody already plays with, doing the one thing the name means.

So the mark is not a drawing of the product — it is the product: the same hood arch, the same face-as-a-hole, the same two eyes and flat brows, the same floating fists, rebuilt from the ghost’s own vector parts in mood-atoms.jsx. Nothing traced.

The pose
HimDefault glow, identity-neutral — no owner’s roll. Eyes level and open, brows flat. Both hands gripping the padded rail with the fingers over the front. Nothing of the body below the rail, and the head just clears it so the eyes are above.
RailPadded band, seam inside it, one thin bright line under it. All three full bleed with square ends: a rail has no ends, a bench does.
ColourOne colour at a time. Teal on the product, gold on burgundy on marketing. No gradient, glow, outline or second colour — the felt tone appears only in the large lockups, where a flat mark loses the table.
How one colour is possible
MaskThe whole mark is an SVG mask filled with a single colour, so every dark part in the reference — the face, the seam, the gap round each fist, the knuckle slits — is a hole, and the background shows through. That is what lets it sit on black, on burgundy, and inside a circular crop without one extra fill.

One decision worth stating

The mark is framed like −hood, not −close.

The three references are the same pose at three distances. −close puts the head at 31% of its frame, which is a poster crop: shrink it to 40px and the head is 12px of hood with the eyes closing. −hood fills its frame, and that is what a mark has to do.

So the canonical mark takes −hood’s framing. −close survives as the lean pose for large surfaces where he should look further away, and −far is the empty room.

The one optical size
GlyphAt 16px the mark’s own eyes are 0.95px tall and the mask antialiases them into the hood. The favicon and the notification therefore use a wider face hole and bigger eyes, and drop the brows. An optical size, not a simplification — they are still ellipses at 16, which is the test the brief set.
