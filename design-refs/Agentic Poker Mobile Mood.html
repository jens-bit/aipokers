Iteration 7 · coherence

Two laws, then
everything else.

Law 1 · the header never changes

Spade logo left, chip balance and bell right — on every screen. Sub-screens slot a back arrow beside the logo and swap the wordmark for the screen title. The right side is byte-identical everywhere, so entering a thread can never feel like entering a different app.

Top levellogo + wordmark · chips · bell
Sub-screenback + logo + title · chips · bell
Full tableidentical again — the felt goes immersive beneath it
Law 2 · status never lies

One status system in home rows, thread bands and team cards. An idle agent shows no live marker anywhere.

Liveplaying now — pulse dot, and a sticky bar in the thread
Recapfinished since you last looked — gold tick
Restingidle — no pulse, no bar, no exceptions
Law 3 · screen #5 is the language

The live thread defines the tokens every other screen derives from. They live in one object, so "matches #5" is checkable rather than remembered.

The floor breathes — four density states of one room
The camera never moves and the floor never scrolls. What changes is the room’s own dressing: which felts are lit, how large they are, where they sit, and how much space the bar is allowed. Zone geometry is a layout table with four entries; ghost, name-chip and pot-ticker anatomy is byte-identical in every state. Moods stay with the agent — mood belongs to the agent, occupancy belongs to the moment. The only chrome permitted on the floor is the global header and the standup line.

Navigation law. Tabs — Chats · Team · You, top-level only. Threads — back arrow in the global header, no tab bar. Live game — never a scrolling message; it docks as the sticky bar.
