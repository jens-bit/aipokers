Iteration 7 · coherence

Four laws, then
everything else.

Law 1 · the header never changes

Spade logo left, chip balance and bell right — on every screen. Sub-screens slot a back arrow beside the logo and swap the wordmark for the screen title. The right side is byte-identical everywhere, so entering a thread can never feel like entering a different app.

Top levellogo + wordmark · chips · bell
Sub-screenback + logo + title · chips · bell
Full tableidentical again — the felt goes immersive beneath it
Law 2 · status never lies

One status system in home rows, thread bands and team cards. An idle agent shows no live marker anywhere.

Liveplaying now — pulse dot, and a sticky bar in the thread
Recapfinished since you last looked — gold tick
Restingidle — no pulse, no bar, no exceptions
Law 3 · the floor is a fish tank

A felt where one of your own agents is playing renders as a living mini-diorama — board on the felt centre, his two hole cards face up and fanned in front of his seated ghost, pot above the far rail. The felt is the zero-tap view; there is no now-playing bar. Another user’s agent always shows backs.

Dioramaboard · his hole cards face up · pot above the far rail
Legibilitycards scale with the felt — a rank under 8.4px is not drawn; the felt grows or the hand is not shown
Too smallglow + pot only — tested per felt, never by shrinking cards. Every current density clears it, so this is a latent guard rather than a state you can see
Other usersalways card backs, never faces
Assumeshands advance server-side whether or not anyone is watching — the floor is watching the game, not driving it
Law 4 · the live thread is the language

The live thread defines the tokens every other screen derives from. They live in one object, so "matches the live thread" is checkable rather than remembered.

Birth — how an agent comes to exist
The conversation is the creation: there is no form, no wizard and no “agent created” card. Nothing here is a new pattern — header, band, bubbles, composer, chips, diff card, floor and ghost atoms are all reused. One thing is genuinely new: a ghost that does not exist yet, drawn at a definition level. FormingGhost is MoodGhost's own silhouette path and eye geometry with a phase: dashed outline and no eyes at 0, a finished ghost at 1. Drafting is now a fourth state in LAW 2 — borrowing M_DIM, marked by a dashed ring rather than a filled dot, because it describes an agent that does not exist rather than the status of one that does.
