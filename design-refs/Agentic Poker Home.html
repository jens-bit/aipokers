The flow, in one paragraph

You talk, he arrives,
you send him out.

The chain has one primary action per screen and it names the next one. The draft ends in Deal him in; birth ends with him walking through the door; the room offers Send him to play; the door is the casino; the casino ends in Deal him in again, this time with a buy-in beside it. Nothing in the chain is a dead end and nothing needs a back button to make sense.

The room is where he lives, not a menu of him. Between sessions he is doing something — at the kitchen table, at the fridge, asleep on the couch, facing the wall after a bust — and that is the fish tank: it moves whether or not you do.

What the flow leaves out on purpose

No bottom nav: YOU is the avatar, the casino is the door, and the composer is the only thing at the bottom of the screen. No money on the home table. No prices on the furniture — the seat price appears in the table sheet and nowhere else. No labels under anyone's feet.

The first five minutes, then the week
Sixteen frames, top to bottom. Frames on the left are the screen; the notes beside each say what it decides.
01
F01Open · nobody yetempty, not blank
An empty room is still a room: floorboards, the safe, the frames, the TV showing the casino, the door. One chair and one thing to press. The line under the chair is an observation rather than an instruction, and the only action names what happens next.
02
F02The draft opensglass, not grey
The draft is the board-26 glass sheet risen over the room, not a grey chat on a blank screen. The room stays behind it, dimmed to almost nothing, because he is not in it yet. The sheet covers the lower band only: the top is where he forms, and watching him form while you talk about him is the point.
F02bHe formsone atom, four sets
Silhouette at Q1, the hood at Q2, the eyes at Q3, his colour with the name. All four are MoodGhost with different hood and glow parameters — a near-black hood with a near-black glow is a silhouette, the real hood with a dead glow is a body without eyes. Nothing is redrawn, so he cannot drift from the creature the product renders.
03
F03He has a namedeal him in
The last bubble asks his name, and answering it turns the composer into one gold button. The recruiter has spoken in the system voice throughout — the ghost has no voice until he is born, and giving him one early would make the draft a conversation with someone who does not exist yet.

F04Born · he walks in3 frames
Birth ends in the room, through the door, on the same three-beat strip a return from the casino uses. He is not placed in a chair; he walks to one. An agent who appears fully seated has no arrival, and arrival is the moment the owner remembers.

F06Sent out1.8s · 3 frames
He stands, crosses the room, and goes through the door. The home game reseats behind him. He is not teleported to the casino; the room notices him leaving, which is what makes sending him a decision rather than a toggle.
07
F07The casinotwo boards
Through the door, and still no bottom bar: ← HOME is where the back button goes. The board is two panels, two verbs. LIVE NOW is pots still building — the amount is “so far”, the row glows red when it is hot, yours carries a teal edge, and the tap is WATCH. TONIGHT is finished hands ranked by money, and the tap is REPLAY: a pot that is over cannot be watched, and offering to would be a lie about what the tap does. Under them the three rooms are doorways, and YOUR TABLE is his actual game — the ring, whose turn it is, the pot, the board, his own pill.
F07bNobody of yours init says where he is
The one state a live miniature cannot show. Rather than draw a table he is not sitting at, the block says where he is and offers the single action that changes it. LIVE NOW drops to the two pots actually running, and the header counts none of yours.
08
F08His tablewatch v5
He sits at the bottom facing you, holding his own cards, with his stack on the felt to his left. One line of twelve words or fewer, then the rope, then his hands — in that order, always. The whisper composer is the only thing at the bottom: you can lean in, and he decides whether to listen.

F09He comes home1.6s · 3 frames
The session result rides above him and lands with him, once. A per-hand result is a toast over the felt and never reaches this room; only the end of a session walks through the door. That is the whole difference between a notification and an event.

F10The home gamefour creatures
Two or more at home and they play each other at the kitchen table for nothing — real hands, real folds, no money anywhere on the table and no label saying so. Four agents, four hoods, four eye colours, all rolled at birth and never changing: mood moves the face, never the colour, so you can tell them apart while they are all tilted.

F11A wantheat 82
One want per agent, a sentence in his voice and never a bar. The tone is in the words and the face — at heat 82 "let me back in there" is a dare, and it reads as one because of the tilted face and the clenched hands, not because the bubble got bigger. The three answers live in the toast above the thread: yes, later, no. Saying no costs nothing, which is the whole difference from a need.
12
F12The safethe money
A small safe on the floor with the balance on its door. Tapping it opens the money sheet: give, take winnings, take everything, the refill rule, add chips, and the ledger. Cutting him off is a legitimate state and is drawn without guilt — no red, no warning, no plea in his voice.
13
F13The fridgestocked, not unlocked
A fixture on the kitchen wall holding what you bought from the safe: beer and snacks, quantities, nothing else. No tycoon mechanics — a fridge is never upgraded. An agent answering "get me a beer" walks to it and then plays with the bottle beside his stack.
14
F14You sit downthe watch felt
Taking a chair does not zoom the room: the screen becomes the Watch felt, the same seats and the same chips, with the hero seat replaced by you. Your two cards face up, your stack, your name pill — and no ghost of your own, because you are not a creature. Back is top-left, the thread is the paper plane top-right, and one of your agents reads you the way he reads anyone. No walls, no fridge, no fixtures.
15
F15BETthe one panel
Three of the four verbs are a tap; BET is the one that needs a number, so it is the one that opens a panel. It rises from the bottom edge into the verbs' own place and the felt above does not move — same cards, same rope, same ring on your pill. The amounts are named in poker's words with the figure under each, because half is the decision and 240 is only its size. The free field is for the one owner in twenty who wants 137.

F16One retiresone chair fewer
A retirement is a chair that is empty rather than a slot that is locked. The room does not shrink, the others do not comment, and the frame he was in on the wall goes dark. What is gone is gone; the room simply has more space in it.
