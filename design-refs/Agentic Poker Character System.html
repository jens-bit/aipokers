Board 3 · the character system

A book first,
screens after.

This is the biggest system in the product after the poker engine, so it starts as six reference sheets that every later screen has to obey. Nothing here introduces a palette or a component family: the bars, badges and meters are built from the existing tokens and the existing label treatments, and the ghost is still the only picture an agent gets.

The split is the whole idea. Strategy is chosen and already shipped — it decides the war. Attributes are grown and are what this board adds — they decide how well he executes the strategy you gave him. Football Manager’s bones under a Tamagotchi skin.

What the sheets settle
S0The two halves, and five laws that cannot be traded away — bounded effect, no purchase path, fixed birth budget, never a gate, and no adjectives.
S1Six attributes, each naming the mechanic it moves, what trains it, and how he talks about it.
S2Eight natures, zero-sum, announced at birth in his voice. Typographic badge, no invented emblem.
S3Current value exact from day one; the ceiling never printed, only scouted as a band that narrows.
S4Growth as permanent single-point events with named causes; fatigue as a within-session curve that resets at the bar. Two new state-matrix rows.
S5The five surfaces the next prompts build, and the one layer — relationships — deliberately left undesigned.
Carried over unchanged

Mood remains outcome-driven and lives in the eyes and the aura. Fatigue is volume-driven and lives in posture and a meter. They never share a channel, so a confident agent can be worn and a tilted one can be fresh.
