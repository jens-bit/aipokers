Design wave 38 · watch v4b

Bubbles,
not a feed.

v4 put his voice in a panel under the felt. Right instinct, wrong place: a feed is a log you read, and what an owner wants is to watch someone talk. So the words move onto the felt as speech bubbles over whoever is speaking, and the panel becomes the record rather than the performance.

Kept from v4 unchanged: the DEAL beat, opponents seated as ghosts with mood posture, tap-a-ghost → read sheet, the 40px header, the tug-of-war, the pace states, the haptic contract.

The bubble law
1One bubble per seat, at most two on the felt, 3–4 seconds, then gone. Never a queue, never a stack — if a third thing is said, the oldest goes.
2His sits above the hero row; an opponent's sits above their own ghost. The tail is the entire mechanism by which you know who spoke.
3Nothing is lost by expiring. The TABLE tab keeps every line, which is exactly what frees a bubble to be brief.
Removed, and replaced

The VOICE tab and the feed, and the decision line under the board. In their place, one tab — TABLE — carrying everything said at this table in order, his lines, theirs and yours, with the composer to talk to him mid-hand. The felt never scrolls; the record always does.

Also new: showdown turns their hole cards face up, in seat order, held, then the pot slides. Backs only while the hand is live — the fish-tank law is unchanged.

Stacking order, as law

The felt is one height for every state — 520px, and its centre is a flow, not a list of offsets. Eleven defects in this wave were one bug repeated: a fixed downstream offset meeting an upstream element whose height depends on state. The heat rope is 36px, not 29. The heat pot pill is 52px, not 39. His ALL-IN bubble is two lines, not one. Each time a constant tuned against one state broke in another.

So pot, board, his bubble band and the rope are one flex column, positioned once at y196 and flowed with 8px gaps above a reserved hero row — a taller pill or a thicker rope now moves its neighbours instead of landing on them. The seats stay absolute because they are anchored to the table, and their bands derive from the seat stack: a seat is 60px (ghost 41 + gap 2 + chip 17) · top row 56 · side band 124 · side row 170. Nothing downstream is a number any more.

The deal, and the bubbles
Two frames of one beat. The interval is the design: 90ms is fast enough to feel like one action and slow enough to feel like two cards — under 60 it reads as a stutter, over 140 as a delay.
