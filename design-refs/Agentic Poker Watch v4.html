Design wave 37

Dealt, seated,
spoken.

v3 fixed tension, stakes and register. What it left behind was a table with nobody at it — five chips carrying names and stacks, no bodies — and reads filed behind a tab nobody opened. This wave adds three things and removes one.

Added
1A DEAL beat before CALM. His cards land one at a time, 90ms apart, one light haptic each, then the table's backs as a single sweep. A premium hand warms his cards in gold — owner-only, never on anyone else's felt.
2Opponents as characters. Every seat is a ghost with a mood posture — House regulars included. Their cards sit behind the shoulder, never in front of the face.
3His voice as the default panel. Decision lines with table talk woven in, and between hands one line of session truth.
Removed

The READ tab. A read is a fact about one opponent, so it lives behind that opponent: tap a ghost, the sheet rises. Five opponents, five reads, and no tab that has to hold six of anything. Also gone: 96px of header chrome, and the MoodBand — whose only load-bearing part was the cause line, which now lives in the between-hands strip where there is room to read it.

Stacking order, as law

rope 276 → line 313 → hero row bottom 12. The decision line is never pinned without a relationship to what sits under it — that was the v3 bug, and on a 420px felt there are exactly 12 pixels of slack between each band.

The deal
Two frames of one beat. The interval is the design: 90ms is fast enough to feel like one action and slow enough to feel like two cards — under 60 it reads as a stutter, over 140 as a delay.
42aDEAL · mid-arrivalone card in
His first card has landed and the second is still travelling — offset 34px, opacity zero, arriving on a 240ms ease. The table has no backs yet: his cards come first, always, because it is his hand you are watching. The rope sits dead centre because there is nothing to weigh yet, and the voice feed carries only the hand number and the stakes.
42bDEAL · a hand worth havingowner-only
Ace-king, and his two cards carry a gold edge. The warm comes straight from pre-flop equity, the hero row's border goes gold, and a small PREMIUM label says why. This is a private read on his own hand — if the screen is ever shared or spectated, both the edge and its soft haptic are suppressed. A gold edge is not a table effect.
42cCALM · six bodiesseated as characters
Five opponents, five postures, and two of them are already out of the hand. Granite is acting — a teal pool under him, breathing. doyle_v3 and ivey_bot have folded and sit at 34% and desaturated, still visibly themselves. nash_eq is another owner's agent: his ghost is fully visible, his cards are backs, and his mood is his own. The gold 3 on Granite is the biography layer, doing its job at 34px.
42dA seat tappedthe READ tab is gone
The read rises from the person it is about. Granite gets a teal ring, the felt drops to 55%, and the sheet carries his five bars, his confidence brackets, his NEMESIS tag, and one line in your agent's voice: "He never folds, so I stop bluffing him." The hand keeps playing behind it — opening a read pauses nothing. Five opponents means five of these, which is exactly why a tab could never have held them.
42eA thin read22 hands
The state that proves the sheet is honest. Twenty-two hands against nash_eq: two bars have values with wide brackets, one has a value and no confidence at all, and two are simply blank with ··. His line refuses to pretend — "Twenty-two hands. He is tight, and that is all I have." A read that fills in evenly regardless of evidence would make the whole system a decoration.
42fHEATINGpot past the threshold
Unchanged from v3, and that is the point — the felt warms, the ticker grows, one rigid tap. What is new is that the postures do not react: a hot pot is his news and the room's, not theirs. The voice feed carries his sizing line as it happens.
42gALL-IN3–5s hold
The one place this product raises its voice, now with witnesses. Red breathing glow, his line in Playfair, RIVER IN 3 in the hero row's tag slot where the timer would be — because there is nothing left to decide. Granite's "Call." sits directly above his line in the feed, which is the whole argument for weaving table talk into it.
42hSHOWDOWNthe table reacts
Every seat reacts in posture, and only in posture. Granite has gone from neutral to tilted; Phil_AI settles to neutral; the folded pair are unmoved, because they were not in it. No badges, no floating numbers, no callouts — the room tells you who lost it by how they are sitting. The pot lifts and fades as the result card lands.
42iBetween handsthe cause line's new home
Where the MoodBand went. The strip carries the cause line — rolling, three big pots in a row — the countdown to the next deal, and one line of session truth: +$3,712 tonight · 43 hands · worst beat: the Q3o on hand 19. That last clause is the sentence a returning owner actually wants, and there was nowhere to put it while a 96px header owned the space.

D1Watch v4 · CALMvoice in the rail
Five bodies around a real oval, and the voice feed docked in the rail with the session-truth line pinned above it. Desktop has no between-hands strip because it has somewhere permanent to put that line. The rail's tabs are the same two — VOICE and CHAT — and the reads are not among them: they are still behind the seats.
D2A seat clickedread in the rail
The read takes the rail rather than rising as a sheet — the one structural divergence between platforms, and it is the right one. Desktop can show the read and the hand at the same time; mobile cannot, so mobile gets a sheet. Granite keeps a teal ring at his seat, the felt drops to 62%, and the hand plays on. The nemesis tag and the lifetime figure fit here because there is room for them.
