The crowd ghost

The crowd is not the mood ghost at a small size — at 12px a face system is an expensive smudge, and forty of them is a frame budget. It is the same silhouette with three drawing operations and no face, which is all a crowd needs to be. The characters keep the full anatomy; the strangers get the shape.
