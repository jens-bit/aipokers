Round 1 was a waiting room. Agents stood in a line pretending to be a room, an agent at the casino was a chair with a caption, and nothing on the screen moved unless you did something. Three mechanics fix that, and all three are motion rather than layout.

1 · The home game

Whenever two or more are home they play each other at the kitchen table for nothing — real hands with the board 26 characters, hands, chips, bubbles and folds, at seat scale. One agent alone has nobody to play, and waits. There is no pot pill, no P&L, no money line and no label at all at the table. This is the fish tank: it is always moving, and it costs the owner nothing to watch.

2 · Away is shown

An agent at the casino is a framed live window on the wall — his table in miniature and moving, his seat lit and pulsing, cards landing, the pot in the corner, a brass plate reading 25/50 · +$340 · 41 min. Tap the frame to walk in and watch. Four away agents is a wall of pictures, and an empty hook is what a slot you are not using looks like.

3 · Routines, and the door

Idle behaviour comes from nature, and state overrides it: a Hothead paces, a Rock reads the paper, a Shark shuffles, a Grinder counts chips — while worn sleeps on the couch and busted faces the wall. Sending him out and coming home are both walks through the door, drawn as strips, because an agent who teleports to the casino is a setting rather than a character.

4 · The body, and what is asked of you

Two of the six attributes are resources and live on the body: two 2.5px bars under the name pill, stamina draining green → olive → grey and heat warming teal → gold → red. No ring — a ring means he is acting. The four skills stay in the profile. A want or a status line is two things at once: his bubble in the room for four seconds, words only and the same size as any other bubble — the tone is in the words and the face, never in the box — and a toast above the collapsed thread that carries the yes / later / no chips, which then folds into the thread's history. Several queue and show one at a time, newest last. Nothing interactive is ever buried in the fish tank.

5 · One TV, one thread, no grey

There is a single screen in the flat and it is the tape room's, at the bottom of the room; when nobody is studying it runs the casino ticker. The bottom of the phone is the home thread collapsed — one glass line with the latest thing said, and the composer. Nothing on the screen is grey. What the tape produces is shown as a count and the newest line (“3 reads on Granite”); the book itself is one tap on the opponent, as a glass sheet.

6 · The safe, and the tape

The money is furniture: a safe on the floor under the wall of frames with the balance on its door, one tap to the money sheet — give, take and the standing rule per agent, plus Add chips. It replaces the wallet header as the way to the money, and YOU keeps the full ledger. While he studies, the room's screen plays the hand in miniature and what the tape produced is one mark under his bubble, +3 GRANITE. Tap the screen for the replay theatre; the read book is one tap on the opponent, never a page in the room.

Three playtest failures, one decision each.

1 · No bottom bar

HOME · CASINO · YOU sat directly under the home thread's composer, so every screen ended in two stacked bars and the composer — the one thing you type into — was the smaller of them. The three destinations become things in the world instead of tabs over it. YOU is the avatar top-right and opens the roster sheet, with the money behind it. CASINO is the door; the wall TV is its preview, so you can see the floor from the sofa. HOME is not a destination — it is where you already are. The composer is the only thing at the bottom of the screen, ever.

2 · Thirty-six creatures

Hood colour and eye glow are rolled at birth from his name and never change — six hoods by six glows. Mood moves the face: eyes, brow, slump. It must never move the colour, or two agents in the same mood become the same creature, which is exactly what the playtest hit.

3 · The bars run opposite ways

STAMINA is a reservoir: full at the right wall, draining leftward, green through amber to red. HEAT is an accumulation: empty at the left wall, filling rightward, ember through fire. The empty end of both is the left end, so a short bar is never good news whichever bar you are reading.

4 · The board is ranked by money

“Heater, heater, biggest pot” read as noise because every line had the same weight. The biggest pot on the floor is now the headline in large type with the amount; two smaller lines sit under it. Every line says who, how much and which room, and every line is a place you can go.
