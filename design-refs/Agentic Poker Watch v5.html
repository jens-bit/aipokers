He sits at
the table.

v4b put him in a hero row — a strip of chrome at the foot of the felt with his cards in it — which made the one character you own the least present thing on his own table. v5 seats him: facing the viewer at the bottom edge at 96px, twice an opponent, cards face up in front, bubble above his head, rope and strip directly under him.

And the felt is the screen. Nothing below it but the composer. The TABLE tab and its transcript are gone — chat is a whisper, and history is a glass sheet over the lower felt with the game still playing behind it.

Two laws this board settles
1You can always see your own cards. When a sheet is open the hero rides over it — cards, rope and strip at full strength, his ghost and bubble at 40% behind the glass. Like holding your cards up over a conversation.
2The hands are in front of the cards. He is holding them, not standing behind them — so the hand layer sits above the card pair rather than inside the ghost.
Why this board exists separately

Board 22 carries v4b's pace states, the glass lower half and the first ceremony. Splitting v5 off keeps both boards light enough to screenshot — 30 frames of live SVG on one canvas was past what the renderer could capture.

Two rows — 40 body + 6 gap + 18 pill = 64px — and two satellites. The timer ring and the dealer button attach to the pill's left edge rather than orbiting the body, so nothing sits on the face. If the seat will not fit, the pill drops to 16px, never the body.
