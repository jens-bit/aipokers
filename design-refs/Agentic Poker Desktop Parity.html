Design wave 39

The states
between the
proofs.

Waves 33–38 were designed mobile-first, and each shipped one or two desktop screens as proof that the layout held. What that left was the states nobody screenshots: the transient ones (DEAL, between hands, collect) and the negative ones (broke, cut off, no history). This wave audits all thirty matrix rows, draws what was missing, and consolidates one desktop matrix.

Nothing on mobile changes. The Command Center furniture is untouched — StandupCard, PGameTile, PComposer and PFlaggedCard are places to put things, not things to reopen.

The audit's answer — · rows
·EXISTS — a desktop component already drew it, because desktop is the same components in a wider frame.
·MAPS — an existing surface carried it with no new component needed.
·MISSING — drawn here, across ten new screens. Several share one: a state and its rail are the same frame.

These three figures are counted from the audit array at render, not written here — a hand-typed tally is a second source of truth.

What desktop has that mobile cannot

Three things, and only three. A permanent truth strip — mobile has to wait for a between-hands moment to say +$3,712 tonight, 43 hands, worst beat the Q3o. The read and the grudge ledger at once — mobile gets a sheet, so it gets one or the other. And three bubbles instead of two, because seats are 170px apart at 852px.

One thing genuinely new

Heat, 0–100 (MOOD-2). Not a mood and not a colour: intensity. It scales the bob and the aura of whatever mood he is already in, so the room reads temperature from how hard the bodies are moving — and the roster is the only place the number is ever exposed, as a 2px hairline in the mood's own colour.
